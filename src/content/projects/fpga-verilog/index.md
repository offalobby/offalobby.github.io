Developed a 16-bit Verilog RTL processor (8-register ISA, ALU, instruction decoder, conditional branching) and Spartan-7 FPGA designs (logic unit, 7-segment decoder, dual-alarm clock), using Vivado, Icarus Verilog testbenches, finite-state machines and clock-enable logic, with the processor integrated on the FPGA.
